SEGMENTATION GUIDANCE
Commercial agreements are segmented by their own numbering. Prefer, in order:
an ARTICLE/SECTION hierarchy (Article V, Section 5.1, 5.1(a)); captioned
provisions in bold or title case ("Limitation of Liability", "Governing Law");
recital blocks beginning WHEREAS; the signature block; and appended Exhibits,
Schedules, and Annexes. Keep a full clause together with its sub-clauses —
splitting 5.1 from 5.1(a) severs a condition from its exception. Treat each
Exhibit or Schedule as its own top-level section, since they frequently carry
pricing and service-level terms.
