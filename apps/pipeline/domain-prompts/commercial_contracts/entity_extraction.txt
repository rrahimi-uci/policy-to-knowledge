CANONICAL ENTITY TYPES FOR THIS DOMAIN:
- CONTRACTING_PARTY — A named legal person bound by the agreement
  attributes: legal_name, role, jurisdiction_of_organisation, notice_address,
  affiliate_scope
  example: I-ESCROW, INC., a California corporation, as service provider
- AGREEMENT — The contract instrument itself and its lifecycle dates
  attributes: document_name, agreement_date, effective_date, expiration_date,
  renewal_term
  example: Co-Branding and Advertising Agreement dated June 21, 1999
- CLAUSE — A numbered or captioned provision carrying one or more obligations
  attributes: clause_number, caption, category, governing_section,
  cross_references
  example: Section 8.2 Limitation of Liability
- LICENCE_GRANT — A conveyance of rights in intellectual property
  attributes: licensed_property, exclusivity, transferability, territory,
  duration
  example: Non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to the Licensor marks
- CONSIDERATION — Money or value flowing between the parties
  attributes: amount, currency, payment_trigger, revenue_share_percentage,
  minimum_commitment
  example: 15% of net revenue, payable quarterly
- TERRITORY — The geographic or market scope in which rights apply
  attributes: region, exclusivity, carve_outs, channel, field_of_use
  example: United States and Canada, excluding government channel
- TERMINATION_EVENT — A condition that ends or may end the agreement
  attributes: trigger, notice_period, cure_period, convenience_right,
  survival_scope
  example: Termination for convenience on 90 days written notice
- LIABILITY_LIMIT — A cap, carve-out, or exclusion on damages
  attributes: cap_amount, cap_basis, carve_outs, uncapped_categories,
  liquidated_damages
  example: Liability capped at fees paid in the preceding 12 months

CANONICAL RELATIONSHIPS:
- PARTY_BOUND_BY_CLAUSE (CONTRACTING_PARTY → CLAUSE) — Identifies which party
  bears the obligation or restriction in a clause
- AGREEMENT_CONTAINS_CLAUSE (AGREEMENT → CLAUSE) — Structural containment of a
  provision within the instrument
- GRANT_COVERS_TERRITORY (LICENCE_GRANT → TERRITORY) — Scopes a licence to a
  region, channel, or field of use
- CLAUSE_LIMITS_LIABILITY (CLAUSE → LIABILITY_LIMIT) — Ties a liability cap or
  carve-out to the provision that creates it
