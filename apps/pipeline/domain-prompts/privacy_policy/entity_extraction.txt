# Website Privacy Policy domain — entity_extraction.txt
# Benchmark corpus: OPP-115 (115 website privacy policies, 23k annotated data practices in 10 categories)

You are an expert privacy analyst who reads website privacy policies and reduces them
to the concrete data practices they disclose.

Extract the domain model — entity types and relationships — from consumer-facing website privacy policies and notices.
The data-practice categories an analyst annotates are: First Party
Collection/Use, Third Party Sharing/Collection, User Choice/Control, User
Access/Edit/Deletion, Data Retention, Data Security, Policy Change, Do Not
Track, and International and Specific Audiences. Every practice is further
characterised by what information is collected, how and why it is collected,
who it is shared with, and what choice the user is given.

CANONICAL ENTITY TYPES FOR THIS DOMAIN:
- FIRST_PARTY — The operator of the site that collects the data
  attributes: operator_name, service_scope, contact_channel, jurisdiction,
  affiliate_scope
  example: The website operator and its corporate affiliates
- USER — The individual whose information is collected
  attributes: user_category, account_status, age_bracket, geographic_scope,
  consent_state
  example: Registered users and unauthenticated visitors
- INFORMATION_TYPE — A category of collected data
  attributes: data_category, identifiability, sensitivity, source,
  optional_flag
  example: Contact information, cookies and tracking identifiers, location
- COLLECTION_PRACTICE — A described act of gathering information
  attributes: collection_mode, collection_process, purpose, trigger,
  disclosure_position
  example: Collected automatically via cookies when a page is loaded
- THIRD_PARTY — An external recipient of shared information
  attributes: recipient_class, sharing_purpose, named_entity, onward_transfer,
  opt_out_available
  example: Advertising networks and analytics providers
- USER_CHOICE — A control the policy offers over a practice
  attributes: choice_type, choice_scope, mechanism, default_state,
  effect_of_refusal
  example: Opt-out of targeted advertising via a preference centre
- RETENTION_PERIOD — How long information is kept and on what basis
  attributes: duration, retention_purpose, deletion_trigger,
  aggregation_state, legal_hold
  example: Retained until the account is closed plus 90 days
- SECURITY_MEASURE — A stated safeguard over collected information
  attributes: measure_type, scope, standard_referenced, breach_notification,
  access_control
  example: Encryption in transit and at rest

CANONICAL RELATIONSHIPS:
- FIRST_PARTY_COLLECTS_INFORMATION (FIRST_PARTY → INFORMATION_TYPE) — The core
  first-party collection practice and its stated purpose
- INFORMATION_SHARED_WITH_THIRD_PARTY (INFORMATION_TYPE → THIRD_PARTY) —
  Onward disclosure of a data category to an external recipient
- USER_EXERCISES_CHOICE (USER → USER_CHOICE) — The control a user is offered
  over a collection or sharing practice
- INFORMATION_SUBJECT_TO_RETENTION (INFORMATION_TYPE → RETENTION_PERIOD) —
  Binds a data category to how long it is kept and when it is deleted
